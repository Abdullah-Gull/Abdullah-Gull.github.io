What are brand guidelines?

Brand guidelines are a roadmap for using your brand effectively. They outline the visual and verbal elements that define your brand, ensuring a consistent and professional experience for your customers.

  • Logo Usage: Proper logo placement, sizing, and color variations.
  • Color Palette: Primary, secondary, and accent colors with hex codes for perfect color consistency.
  • Typography: Defined fonts for headlines, body text, and other elements to maintain a unified look.
  • Imagery Style: Preferred photography styles, illustrations, and graphic elements that reflect your brand personality.
  • Voice and Tone: Guidelines for the language and tone you use in your marketing materials and customer interactions.
