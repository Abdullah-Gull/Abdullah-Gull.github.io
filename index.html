Scalable marketplace growth requires both paid advertising and organic optimization. The following is a breakdown of how Abdullah Gull leverages every strategy to maximize your revenue.

Amazon PPC is a strategic paid model where you only pay when a potential buyer clicks your ad. Through precision-targeted bidding, I position your products on the first page instantly. This approach generates predictable sales velocity and allows us to scale your revenue with total financial control.

Amazon SEO

Amazon SEO is the art of ranking your listings organically. I integrate high-intent keywords optimized for the A9 algorithm to ensure your catalog is indexed perfectly. This builds long-term brand authority and delivers consistent, free traffic, significantly boosting your overall profit margins.
