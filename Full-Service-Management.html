Catalog Troubleshooting

Amazon can be complicated to navigate, even for seasoned sellers. Add in policy updates and notoriously bad seller support, and it can get troublesome quickly. With over five years of troubleshooting experience, I'm confident I can help.

Price Strategy

There are a variety of pricing strategies that can be used to attract customers, increase sales, and maintain competitiveness — among them dynamic pricing, competitive pricing, bundling, promotions, and subscriptions.

Parentages

By setting up parentages, we connect variations such as color or size to the same listing. This gives customers improved options to purchase. Parentage setup or recreation involves a complex process that requires you to have familiarity with Amazon flat files — I run this the right way to reduce the risk of deleting your Amazon catalog data.

New Product Creation

Adding new products to Amazon's catalog has never been easier. New problems can often get lost in the process of gaining approvals, using the right product identifiers, submitting the necessary documents, and correctly filling all the fields required by Amazon — luckily, with your own specialist handling the complexity, you're free to focus on growing your business.

UPC to GS1 Barcode Changes

Amazon requires sellers to use GS1 UPC codes to be fully compliant and prevent listings from disappearing. I'll update these to stay compliant and prevent problems before they happen.

Negative Seller Feedback

Once a single star review hits your account, I'll do my best to remove it. Please note that we say "attempt" as it's a numbers game, but I can't promise every review gets removed, we can confidently say that many of them will.

Brand Name Changes

A brand name change can occur for many reasons. You might need to do it as a result of optimizing your catalog or adding a newly registered brand. Unfortunately, Amazon doesn't make it easy to change your brand name — sometimes this simple task can lead to hours of work. Luckily, if you're a newly registered seller, you might run into problems connecting your listings to your brands, I prevent this from happening — and if you're already selling using A+ content or brand stores, I know how to deal with tricky brand name changes and brand-side issues, so you can get peace of mind.

Listing Reinstatement

Every Amazon seller will eventually deal with a listing suspension or takedown. If your top-selling ASIN has been deactivated, or you're seeing a "Page not found" error, you'll need a reliable specialist to get your listing back up. I have years of experience with reinstating listings on Amazon — I know exactly what steps to take, and how to construct and submit an effective Plan of Action (POA) that meets Amazon's requirements to get your listing back up and running. The faster we get your listing back online, the faster you'll make sales again.

Other Amazon Issues

Seller Central changes and grows every day. With that come bugs and errors that can affect your listings and ultimately your sales. I know how to fix virtually any error on Seller Central — from a broken categorization that can lead to a drop in search rankings to image bugs that can suppress your listing. I can also fix tricky A+ Content or Store rejection errors, get items suspended from advertising running again, remove poor seller feedback that can hurt your buy box rating, and get rid of nearly every other common error that comes with selling on Amazon.
