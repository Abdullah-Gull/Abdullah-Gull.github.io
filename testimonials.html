The Challenge

Understanding Nescafy's Position

Nescafy, a premium beverage brand with strong offline presence and brand recognition, faced significant challenges when scaling on Amazon. Despite having quality products and customer loyalty in traditional retail, their Amazon channel wasn't reflecting their brand strength or market potential.

  • PPC campaigns running at 28% ACOS with suboptimal keyword strategies
  • Fragmented product listings across multiple SKUs without unified messaging
  • Poor organic visibility for high-intent keywords despite strong brand authority
  • Limited A+ content and enhanced brand content across product catalog
  • Competitive pressure from generic brands undercutting on price
  • Minimal customer reviews and social proof compared to competitors

Our Strategy

Multi-Layered Approach to Amazon Domination

We developed a comprehensive strategy addressing every aspect of Nescafy's Amazon presence. Our approach focused on data-driven decision making, brand positioning, and sustainable growth rather than short-term wins.

Advanced PPC Architecture

Restructured campaigns into granular SKU groups, implemented bid automation rules, and integrated customer journey mapping for precision targeting across awareness and conversion stages.

SEO & Keyword Optimization

Performed deep market analysis, identified long-tail opportunities, and optimized all product listings for A9 algorithm with keyword density, placement strategy, and backend search term optimization.

Brand Content Enhancement

Created premium A+ content with lifestyle imagery, product benefits visualization, and trust-building elements. Designed brand store experience to strengthen brand narrative across all touchpoints.

Review Generation Program

Implemented ethical review generation strategies, established follow-up sequences, and built social proof elements to increase conversion rates and improve product rankings organically.

Competitive Analysis & Positioning

Analyzed top 20 competitors, identified market gaps, and repositioned Nescafy as the premium choice through messaging, pricing strategy, and unique value proposition highlighting.

Performance Monitoring & Optimization

Set up comprehensive dashboards, implemented weekly optimization cycles, and conducted monthly strategy reviews to ensure continuous improvement and rapid adaptation to market changes.
