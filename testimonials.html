E-commerce Background

Nescafe, as an iconic global leader in the beverage sector, recognized the need to maximize its operational velocity inside the Amazon US Seller & Vendor systems. Despite unparalleled global brand equity, the digital marketplace version suffered from inconsistent listings visibility and fragmented keyword rankings.

The objective was clear: build a robust, customized e-commerce framework capable of aligning their premium asset quality with advanced marketplace execution. Abdullah Gull stepped in to engineer a custom optimization engine focused on conversion, visibility retention, and multi-tier repurchase loops.

Structural Marketplace Weaknesses Identified

A9 Algorithm Listing Friction

Core listings lacked granular indexing, missing key search terms for single-origin and premium blends.

Sub-Optimal Visual Conversion Assets

Hero imagery failed to convey the premium sensory nature of the coffee, resulting in elevated drop-off rates.

PPC Cannibalization Loops

Overlapping ad campaigns were bidding against historical brand data, drastically inflating ACOS limits unnecessarily.
