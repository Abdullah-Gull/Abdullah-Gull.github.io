The Challenge

Understanding Nescafy's Market Position

Nescafy, a trusted premium beverage brand with strong offline presence and consumer loyalty, faced significant challenges scaling their Amazon business. While they enjoyed brand recognition in traditional retail channels, their Amazon performance didn't reflect their market strength or growth potential.

The brand struggled with fundamental e-commerce challenges that prevented them from capturing market share on the world's largest online retail platform.

  • PPC campaigns operating at inefficient 28% ACOS with unoptimized keyword strategies and poor bid management
  • Product listings scattered across multiple SKUs without cohesive messaging, optimization, or A+ content
  • Poor organic search visibility for high-intent, high-traffic keywords despite strong brand authority
  • Minimal premium A+ content and enhanced brand experience across the product catalog
  • Intense competitive pressure from generic brands using aggressive pricing tactics
  • Limited customer reviews and social proof compared to competitors, affecting conversion rates
  • No structured review generation program or customer feedback strategy

Our Strategy

Multi-Layered Amazon Growth Strategy

We designed a comprehensive, data-driven strategy addressing every critical aspect of Nescafy's Amazon business. Our approach focused on sustainable, scalable growth through systematic optimization, brand positioning, and customer-centric strategies.

Advanced PPC Architecture

Restructured campaigns into granular SKU groups, implemented intelligent bid automation, and integrated customer journey mapping for precision targeting across awareness and conversion stages. Reduced ACOS while maintaining volume growth.

Expert Amazon SEO

Conducted in-depth keyword research, identified long-tail opportunities, and systematically optimized listings for A9 algorithm with strategic keyword placement, density optimization, and backend search term strategies.

Premium Brand Content

Designed compelling A+ content with lifestyle imagery, product benefit visualization, trust elements, and brand storytelling. Enhanced brand store experience to strengthen narrative across all customer touchpoints.

Ethical Review Generation

Developed compliant review generation program, implemented strategic follow-up sequences, and built authentic social proof elements. Increased ratings while maintaining Amazon policy compliance.

Competitive Intelligence

Analyzed top competitors, identified market gaps, and repositioned Nescafy as premium choice. Developed unique positioning, pricing strategy, and value proposition highlighting brand advantages.

Continuous Optimization

Established comprehensive performance dashboards, implemented weekly optimization cycles, conducted monthly strategy reviews, and enabled rapid adaptation to market dynamics and emerging opportunities.
