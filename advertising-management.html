Keyword strategies

I A/B/C test keywords in the three formats below — a keyword could perform better in any one of the below formats, and I adjust bids accordingly.

01

Broad Match

Generically targets the keyword, can have other words in the search results mixed in. Generates the most impressions.

02

Broad Phrase

Must contain the phrase, can have other words in the search result.

03

Exact Match

Is exact match, and only targets when precise letter for letter keywords are used. Least amount of impressions, but most controlled and targeted format.

Sponsored Products

Increase visibility and drive product sales with Sponsored Product ads that appear in Amazon search results and product detail pages.

Brand campaign

  1. Brand terms are only used in this campaign
  2. Defensive advertising, not incremental sales, low spend
  3. Protects you from competitors advertising on your brand

Competitor brand campaign

  1. Competitor brand names are only used in this campaign
  2. Targets competitor's brand keywords in order to gain market share
  3. Small spend, high results, low impression counts, incremental sales

Competitor ASIN targeting

  1. Selective ASINs we want our product to show up on their detail pages
  2. Weaker-performing search terms are redirected to specific ASINs

Defensive ASIN targeting campaign

  1. Target ASINs from your own catalog
  2. Places your ads on the detail page of your own products
  3. Keeps competitors off of your page while advertising more of your catalog to increase conversion and get some splash sales

Video ads

  1. Hot and in demand
  2. Requires professional short videos, recommended length: 30 seconds
  3. Requires Brand Registry

Sponsored display

  1. Retargeting
  2. Past purchasers
  3. Category and ASIN targeting display

Auto campaign

  1. Medium spend keywords chosen by Amazon, good for keyword discovery in case manual campaigns miss an opportunity
  2. Brand terms are negated
  3. Substitutes: I show your ad to shoppers who view the detail pages of products similar to yours
  4. Complements: I show your ad on the detail page of your own products
  5. Loose match: I show your ad to shoppers who use search terms loosely related to your products
  6. Close match: I show your ad to shoppers who use search terms closely related to your products

Manual campaign

  1. Highly segmented ad groups have the most benefit, most spend, and most sales
  2. Keywords are all manually chosen
  3. I use a bid modifier to bid higher for top of search placements
  4. Testing has shown using less than 50 keywords on manual campaigns is more effective than loading hundreds
