Mastering Amazon Global Selling: How to Crush ACOS and Scale Profits

By: Abdullah Gull Amazon Private Label Expert

Selling on Amazon is easy; staying profitable is the real battlefield. Whether you are navigating the high-competition landscape of the USA or the rapidly growing markets of the UK and UAE, controlling your Advertising Cost of Sales (ACOS) is the ultimate key to success.

I’ve managed numerous accounts across these international marketplaces, and today I’m sharing the exact framework I use to keep ACOS low and ROI high.

1. Global Keyword Strategy: Localize, Don't Just Translate

Every market searches differently. If you use the same keywords everywhere, you are leaving money on the table.

  • USA vs. UK: In the USA, customers search for "Sweater," but in the UK, they search for "Jumper".
  • UAE Market: This requires a strategic mix of both English and Arabic search terms.
Pro Tip: Never use a "one-size-fits-all" list. I personally localize every PPC campaign to match local search behavior.

2. Negative Keyword Pruning: The "Budget Leak" Fix

Your ACOS skyrockets when your ads show up for irrelevant searches that take your clicks but never convert.

Every single week, I analyze the Search Term Report for my clients. Any keyword with 8-10 clicks and zero sales is immediately moved to Negative Exact Match.

3. Dynamic Bidding (Down Only): Smart Protection

I always recommend starting with "Dynamic Bids - Down Only". This tells Amazon to only spend your money when the chance of a sale is high, protecting your budget from "window shoppers".

4. Listing Optimization: The Foundation

High-quality A+ Content (EBC) isn't just "pretty pictures"—it builds brand authority. This can boost your conversion rate by up to 15%, which directly pulls your ACOS down.

Final Verdict by Abdullah Gull

Success in Amazon Global Selling is hidden in the data. By monitoring analytics and performing weekly optimizations, no market is too difficult to conquer.
